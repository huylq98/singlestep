# About Singlestep

Singlestep is a personal engineering knowledge base built around **interactive simulations**.
Instead of static diagrams and wall-of-text explanations, each non-trivial topic gets a small
toy you can play with: toggle a setting, watch the system respond, build intuition by doing.

## Why this exists

Most engineering content is one of two things:

1. Reference docs that tell you *what* a thing does.
2. Blog posts that tell you *that someone learned* a thing.

There's a third option that doesn't get built often because it's expensive: **interactive
explanations that let you see exactly how a system behaves under different inputs.** That's
what this site is.

## How it's organized

- **Docs** — evergreen reference notes. Editable forever.
- **Blog** — dated posts about experiences, debugging stories, lessons.
- **Playground** — a gallery of every simulation, browsable on its own.
