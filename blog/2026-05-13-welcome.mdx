This is the first post on Singlestep — a personal engineering knowledge base built
around interactive simulations.

{/* truncate */}

The first hero demo is [Redis read modes](/docs/redis/read-modes). Toggle between
master and replica reads and watch where the requests go. It's the kind of thing that
takes ten minutes to read about and three seconds to understand once you can see it.

More to come — Kafka consumer rebalancing, cache eviction policies, transaction
propagation. If a topic confused you the first time you learned it, that's exactly
the kind of thing that gets a sim here.
